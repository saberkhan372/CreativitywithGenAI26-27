## Connect it back

BookBot drew from counts you made by hand from one passage. These systems do
something recognisably related — predicting what comes next — fitted to a quantity
of text no person could read in a lifetime.

That is the honest version of the comparison. What is **not** the same: BookBot had
no way to be steered, and no way to refuse. These do. That difference is where the
rest of the course lives.
