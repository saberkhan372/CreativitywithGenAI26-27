Working with Gen AI · 2026–27

How machines
handle language

Five 70-minute lessons about explicit rules, counted patterns, and learned prediction — and about the judgement a person still has to supply.

The spine: rules → counts → learned prediction, with fluency is not accuracy carried across all five days.

Every lesson runs the same four parts: a warm-up question, the lesson activity, an in-class reflection and Classroom submission, and a preview of the next lesson. The decks carry the lesson; the two-page handouts are reference sheets, not required worksheets. Decks include teacher notes and need no downloads, accounts, or logins.
