Lesson 3 · Step 1Example only — yours goes on the board
Our whole book

Six lines. That is the entire library.

the fox   paints a moon .

Rule 1

Count every time, including repeats. Line 6 repeats line 1. That repeat is not a mistake to tidy up — it is the pattern.

Rule 2

The period counts as a word. It is how you will know when to stop.

Step 3 · walk it · 12 minutes

Always take the most common word.

Start at the. Look at the book. Whichever word follows it most often — write that down. Then do it again from that word. Stop when you write the period.

Step 4 · the catch

You all wrote the same sentence.

the robot paints a moon .

That is line 1

It is also line 6. The most likely sentence turned out to be the one the book repeats.

Run it again

You get the same sentence. And again. It can only ever make one.

What would it need to write a different one?

Lesson 3 · Step 4Hold that answer — Unit 2
Back to the cat question

Why does moon win?

Not because it is a better word. Because it is in the book three times out of six.

"The same cat a million times"

What does that detector learn? That cat. Not cats.

"Keep the cat in the middle"

Then what happens to a cat at the edge of the frame?

What goes in decides what comes out.
