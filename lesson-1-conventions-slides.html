Lesson 1 · Warm-upHandout Part A
What happened

Nobody has ever written those rules.

  • Every rule you wrote needs another rule underneath it.
  • Every rule you wrote has a photo that breaks it.
  • Face unlock, spam filters, autocomplete — none were built this way.

So how were they built?

Lesson 1 · Debrief
The distinction

Two ways to build

Rule-written

  • A person writes every rule
  • Builder supplies the logic
  • You can read it afterward
  • Weird input → breaks, or refuses

Learned

  • Nobody writes the rules
  • Builder supplies examples
  • You mostly cannot read it
  • Weird input → confident nonsense

That last row is the one that will matter all semester.

Lesson 1 · Part BDashed = arguable
Class conventions · all semester

How we will work

1
Log the exact thing.

Prompt and output verbatim. A retyped output is not evidence.

2
Observation before interpretation.

"One sentence was false" ≠ "it lied." Say which you mean.

3
One variable at a time.

Two changes at once tells you nothing about either.

4
Name the authorship.

What you decided, what it produced. Every time.

5
Consent and privacy.

No real names, faces, or private details into any tool. Ever.

6
Participation is not obligation.

Observing, recording, questioning, declining — all full participation.

One bounded claim

I observed
My evidence is
This suggests
but it does not establish

The fourth clause is the whole point. Anyone can make a claim. Naming its limit is what makes it honest.
